    url: "https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.23899"

    description: >-
      We train a team of heterogeneous UAVs to coordinate and guide human evacuees to
      safety in an urban fire scenario. We model the problem as a POMDP where humans have
      unknown panicked behavior and use reinforcement learning to learn adaptive policies
      for guiding evacuees.

    url: "https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.05812"
    authors: ["A. Kalanther", "D. Bostwick", "C. Maheshwari", "S. Sastry"]
    venue: "Workshop on Multi-Agent Systems, Robotics: Science and Systems (RSS)"
    year: 2025
    links:
      - label: project page
        href: "https://sastry-group.github.io/PEG/"
      - label: arXiv
        href: "https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.05812"
    description: >-
      We develop a sample-efficient adaptation strategy to handle unknown adversaries in a
      partially-observable pursuit-evasion game. We use a bounded rationality model to collect
      diverse and sophisticated evader policies and train an adaptive policy for the pursuer
      team using deep RL.

I am a researcher in [Berkeley Artificial Intelligence Research](https://bair.berkeley.edu/) at [UC Berkeley](https://www.berkeley.edu/), advised by Professor [Shankar Sastry](https://www2.eecs.berkeley.edu/Faculty/Homepages/sastry.html). I work on reinforcement learning methods for multi-agent games with unknown adversaries. I graduated with my bachelor's degree in [EECS](https://www.eecs.berkeley.edu/) from UC Berkeley in Winter 2024. I previously worked as a software engineer, interning at [Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/), [Lucid Software](https://www.lucid.co/), and [BeamNG](https://www.beamng.com/).

I am currently applying to PhD programs in robotics and AI. If you are interested in my research, please reach out to me.
